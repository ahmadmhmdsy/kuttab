## <ISO-8601 with offset> — <title>

**State.** Where the project is right now, in two or three sentences. Assume
the reader has read nothing else.

**Did.** What this session actually changed. Paths as path:line. Be specific
about what was applied, what was committed, and what was left uncommitted.

**Verified.** What was checked, with the exact command and its exact result.
Use PASS / FAIL / SKIPPED labels. Do not write "verified" without saying how.

**Unverified / open.** What was not checked and what remains uncertain. This
section is usually the most valuable one for the next session.

**Next.** The single next step. One step, not a list.

**Blockers.** What is gated and on what. Write "none" rather than omitting it.

**Artifacts.** Paths worth opening first.
